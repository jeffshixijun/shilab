Work with us on the next generation of infrastructure materials.

SHI Lab welcomes motivated graduate students, undergraduate researchers, postdoctoral researchers, and collaborators whose interests align with our research.

Prospective graduate students

Strong applicants typically have backgrounds in civil engineering, materials science, structural/materials engineering, pavement engineering, chemistry, mechanics, or related areas. Experience with experimental research, cementitious materials, modeling, or data analysis is valuable.

When contacting the lab, include a concise introduction, research interests, CV/resume, academic background, and the degree/program you are interested in.

Collaborations & visitors

We collaborate with universities, government agencies, industry, and technology-transfer partners on sustainable construction materials and infrastructure applications.

For research collaborations, visiting scholar opportunities, or technical discussions, please contact Dr. Shi directly.
