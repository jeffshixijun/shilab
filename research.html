Research

Infrastructure materials for a resource-constrained future.

SHI Lab research combines sustainability, mechanics, durability, materials characterization, and implementation.

01

Recycled Materials in Concrete

Recycled concrete aggregate, reclaimed asphalt pavement, waste glass, eggshell powder, recycled tire steel fibers, and other secondary materials are evaluated for mechanical performance, durability, and life-cycle benefits.

02

UHPC & Fiber Reinforcement

We develop lower-cost, more sustainable UHPC and fiber-reinforced cementitious composites, with emphasis on recycled steel fibers, fracture behavior, toughness, overlays, bridges, pavements, and industrial applications.

03

Biopolymer Construction Materials

Reversible biopolymer binders enable a new class of recyclable construction materials aimed at temporary structures, water recovery, material reuse, and construction in resource-limited environments.

04

Lunar & Martian Construction

Our extraterrestrial construction research investigates regolith-based geopolymers, fiber reinforcement, processing, and additive manufacturing using in-situ resources.

05

Durability & Coastal Infrastructure

Research addresses cracking, shrinkage, ASR, freeze-thaw behavior, and durable materials for transportation and coastal infrastructure.

06

Pavement Materials & Modeling

Our work includes concrete and asphalt pavement materials, thermal behavior, reclaimed materials, fracture, rutting, performance modeling, and sustainable pavement design.
